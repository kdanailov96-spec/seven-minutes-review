What modularity does - and does not - protect against

Operational refusal

Separate tiers protect against ordinary friction: no room, schedule full, wrong timing, one team declining. A narrow route can survive even if another tier dies.

Association refusal

Modularity cannot solve a top-level decision that EBU / Host Broadcaster is not comfortable with the association. That risk must be surfaced early, not routed around.

Two operations, not four

Contest-period delivery and the post-May youth programme should be resourced and led separately. The second has a different timeline, legal environment, safeguarding burden and institutional route.

04 — Duty of Care Protocol review

What the protocol changes for us

Positioning

Eurovision already has substantial wellbeing provision

The reviewed protocol covers psychological readiness, optional independent psychological assessment, access to professional psychological support before / during / after the event, on-site specialist support where appropriate, rest and post-event after-care.

Implication

Never sell this as filling a gap

They do not need us. The defensible position is one additional, optional, non-clinical practice that may sit alongside existing professional support - not a correction to Eurovision's welfare system.

Delegation ownership

Heads of Delegation matter more than v0.2 suggested

The protocol makes HoDs the authorised representatives responsible for their organisation's participation and duty of care. They are the primary point of contact with the EBU for delegation matters.

Different in kind

No meditation or mindfulness practice is explicitly specified

No explicit meditation, mindfulness, relaxation or self-care practice appears in the protocol text reviewed. That is not evidence of unmet need; it only means our proposed practice is different from the measures explicitly described.

Voluntary principle

Our opt-in design fits the protocol's logic

The protocol emphasises informed participation, individual autonomy and the right to withdraw. Our internal rule should remain: always offered, never applied.

2027 unknown

Host-specific measures come later

The protocol says EBU / Host Broadcaster-specific measures are to be described separately closer to the event once host production and organisation are known. Tier 2 should be revalidated against the 2027 implementation material.

Source-status caveat: the detailed text reviewed is Duty of Care Protocol v1.1 (December 2024), retrieved through a public mirror of the EBU document. The current public 2027 Rules still explicitly incorporate an EBU Duty of Care Protocol. No newer full public version surfaced in this review; re-check immediately before outreach.
