01 — Executive decision

What are we actually considering?

Working proposition

Make a free seven-minute meditation available across selected parts of the Eurovision 2027 ecosystem in Burgas - staffed and operated by us, with no funding requested - and build a separate post-May practice programme for young people only if the legal and safeguarding route is sound.

Recommendation

No figurehead moment during Contest week

No Sadhguru appearance, no CMC-owned figurehead event and no attempt to create a “guru at Eurovision” moment. Keep this as a strategic guardrail unless formal EBU guidance gives a compelling reason to revisit it.

Correction after protocol review: this is a strategic association / neutrality / reputational judgement, not a verified standalone “religious neutrality rule”.

Duty of Care update

The core permission question is still open

The protocol defines extensive welfare responsibilities, but it does not say whether an external, non-clinical wellbeing practice may operate inside accredited areas. That question still needs a direct EBU answer.

What we are not asking for

No funding. No stage time. No broadcast presence. No Eurovision brand or logo rights. No official partner designation. No category exclusivity. No artist introductions. No press support. No commitment beyond 2027. No obligation to continue anything that is not working.

Important: “No funding requested” is credible. “Zero cost” is not. Any organiser still carries real non-financial cost: time, access, legal / contracting review, scheduling, space, security and reputational responsibility.
02 — Strategic model

Inside the Contest, or alongside it

The distinction matters more after reading the protocol because artist and delegation welfare has defined owners.
Upside route

Inside - accredited

Crew / volunteer and delegation-facing activity inside the official ecosystem.

  • Requires accreditation and event permission.
  • Needs an internal broadcaster / EBU route.
  • Delegation participation remains with each Participating Broadcaster / Head of Delegation.
  • Buys access to the people under the most pressure.
  • Costs institutional dependency and a real chance of a non-appealable no.
Base case

Alongside - municipal

A Burgas wellbeing programme delivered outside official Eurovision-branded space.

  • No accreditation required for an ordinary independent city activity.
  • No implied Eurovision partnership or endorsement.
  • Can begin before Contest week and continue after May.
  • No access to delegations / crew as part of this route.
  • Its lack of official association is also its main protection.
Recommendation

Pursue both, but treat the adjacent municipal model as the base case rather than the consolation prize. Accredited access is upside, not the foundation of the project.

A critical split inside Tier 3
Independent city programme

Outside official Eurovision-branded space

A Burgas programme in ordinary public city spaces can be designed without claiming or implying Eurovision association. This is the cleanest fallback route, subject to normal municipal permissions.

Official Village route

Inside Eurovision Village is different

Eurovision Village is part of the official event ecosystem. Current 2027 rules reserve official association and brand use to authorised parties, so a Village activation cannot be treated as municipality-only permission.

Correction to v0.2: “municipal” and “Eurovision Village” are not one permission route. The independent city programme is the true fallback; an official Village activation is an upside requiring event approval.
03 — Programme · accredited tiers

Four permissions, two operations

The tiers are a permission map. Operationally, the project is two things: a Contest-period operation in Burgas and a separate post-May youth programme with different law, safeguarding and ownership.

1

Crew & volunteers

Optional short sessions for BNT production staff, contractors and the city volunteer corps, at induction and through build / event weeks.

Working route · HYPOTHESIS
BNT HR / workforce owner and Burgas volunteer programme manager. These operational owners still need to be identified and verified.
What we need
A named contact, an optional induction mention, and a suitable shared space if feasible.
PROTOCOL: the Duty of Care Protocol is primarily artist / delegation-facing. Tier 1 remains a separate workforce wellbeing proposition.
2

Delegations

Request scheduled use of existing quiet / welfare space and, if permitted, visibility through an approved delegation information channel. Keep sessions short, voluntary and onsite.

Event route
EBU ESC delegation / event team for logistics and official channels; each Participating Broadcaster / Head of Delegation decides for its own delegation.
Welfare Producer
Relevant liaison for artist and staff welfare. The reviewed protocol does not state that the Welfare Producer has a formal veto over third-party offers.
PROTOCOL: Heads of Delegation are the primary EBU contact and carry duty-of-care responsibility for their delegation.
Operational implication

Delegations are expected to follow official hotels, transport and rehearsal schedules. An onsite, short, shared-space session is therefore more credible than an offsite activation requiring additional travel during Contest week.
