07 — Risks and guardrails

Only the risks that can change the decision

Association / neutrality

The current ESC framework explicitly protects the Contest's non-political character. Association with a spiritual / advocacy ecosystem may still create reputational discomfort even if delivery is secular. This is a real “no” risk, not something wording alone can solve.

Existing welfare system

The protocol already provides meaningful psychological and welfare measures. Never imply Eurovision lacks support. Our offer is additional and removable, not corrective.

Official association / brand

No external entity may imply Eurovision partnership or endorsement without authorisation. The independent city base case must stay visibly unbranded unless permission changes.

Delegation gatekeeping

EBU can enable logistics, but Participating Broadcasters / HoDs own their delegations. Tier 2 is not one approval.

Data protection

If Miracle of Mind is recommended through an organiser channel, the app's controller, hosting, transfers, retention, lawful basis and minors position need a one-page DPO-ready answer before formal conversation.

Youth safeguarding

The post-May youth tier requires its own safeguarding, consent, insurance and legal route. Eurovision's protocol does not solve those Bulgarian obligations for us.

Operational burden

“No funding requested” is true; “zero cost” is not. Access, legal review, scheduling, rooms, security and someone's attention are real organiser costs.

07 — Artist track · DARA

Keep the artist track separate from the institutional proposal

Binding rules
  • Approach through management only. Never directly, never at an event.
  • First contact contains an offer and no ask.
  • No health disclosure appears in material we produce unless she has approved that specific sentence.
  • She speaks in her own words or not at all. We write nothing for her.
  • She can withdraw at any point, including after an announcement, with no consequence and no follow-up.
  • Never mention her to EBU, BNT or the city as leverage.
  • If she declines, the answer is thank you and the subject is closed.

Why keep this separate?

Artist participation is personal and consent-based; it should never be used to unlock institutional approval. The Eurovision proposal must stand on its own even if no artist ever associates publicly with the programme.

08 — Critical unknowns

What still blocks a serious external proposal

01 · PERMISSION
Can a third-party non-clinical wellbeing offer operate inside accredited areas at all?

The protocol does not answer this. Get the EBU answer before designing Tier 2 around assumptions.

02 · 2027 IMPLEMENTATION
What host-specific welfare measures will EBU / BNT issue for Burgas?

The protocol says event-specific measures are described separately closer to the event.

03 · CAPACITY
How many session-capable leaders can actually deliver in BG and EN?

A credible proposition needs a number, not generic volunteer enthusiasm.

04 · NETWORK
Who can make a credible warm introduction?

Map real personal routes into BNT, Burgas, EBU-adjacent teams and the music industry.

05 · DATA
What is Miracle of Mind's data posture?

Controller, hosting, transfers, retention, lawful basis and minors. Prepare a DPO-ready one-pager.

06 · ENTITY
Which legal person contracts and carries liability?

A volunteer group cannot be the contracting answer for a public broadcaster.

07 · PRECEDENT
What exactly may we say about UNTOLD?

Secure clearance and, ideally, a named reference before using it externally.

08 · CONFLICT
Are there health / wellness / app-category partner conflicts?

Public research may not reveal active commercial conversations. Ask early.

Kill conditions
  • No credible introduction / route into the relevant organisations by the agreed internal deadline.
  • Insufficient trained session-leader capacity for Contest-period delivery.
  • EBU confirms accredited external wellbeing activity is not permissible: stop accredited tiers and continue only with the independent city route.
