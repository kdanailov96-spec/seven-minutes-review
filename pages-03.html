06 — Outreach route

Make the first contact small enough to answer

0
EBU ESC team - first, but not with a proposal

Five lines. Who we are; that we are exploring an unbranded Burgas city programme and a separate optional crew / delegation wellbeing offer; that we want to work inside the rules; and one question: is a third-party, non-clinical wellbeing practice inside accredited areas permissible in principle, and who owns that question?

1
Burgas Municipality - build the base case

Speak to the volunteer and city-programme team. Start with a useful Burgas programme, not a grand Eurovision partnership narrative.

2
BNT - crew wellbeing through the correct owner

Identify the actual workforce / HR owner first. Keep the ask operational: a contact, an optional induction mention and suitable shared space if feasible.

3
Delegation route - only after permission is clear

Use the ESC delegation team to understand what official information channel and space are actually available. Then individual Participating Broadcasters / HoDs decide whether their delegation participates.

4
Legacy institutions - later

Ministry / education conversations only after safeguarding, legal entity and data posture are ready.

5
Formal EBU ask - last

Carried by evidence that we can deliver, ideally with BNT / Burgas context rather than as a cold top-level pitch.

Attrition rule

One follow-up at 10 working days, one at 25. Then record silence as a no. No third attempt and no going around the person.

Warm introductions

Public contact details are not the strategy. The highest-value research task remains finding credible routes through our own network into BNT, Burgas, EBU-adjacent teams and the Bulgarian music industry.

06 — Strategic precedent

What transfers from UNTOLD

Keep UNTOLD's logic - a simple free practice, offered voluntarily, delivered by us, extending beyond the event. Drop UNTOLD's theatre.

What transfers

  • Simple, low-friction practice.
  • Voluntary participation.
  • We carry delivery responsibility.
  • Multiple storytelling / participation surfaces.
  • A before / during / after horizon.
  • Potential legacy beyond the event.

What does not transfer

  • Main-stage figurehead moment.
  • One-person decision-maker speed.
  • Assumed brand freedom.
  • Festival-style tolerance for spectacle.
  • Unmediated artist access.
  • Celebrity-facing moments that delegation protocol does not permit us to promise.
Structural difference: UNTOLD is a privately controlled commercial festival. Eurovision is a public-service broadcast ecosystem with formal governance, broadcaster responsibilities, accreditation, brand controls and a much more constrained delegation environment. The partnership logic may transfer; the execution architecture does not.
